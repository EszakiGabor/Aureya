## Overview

Északi Gábor (Seza) is a Hungarian origami artist, educator and system builder active since 2008.

His work combines modular thinking, structural design, symmetry and spatial logic. Across both physical and digital mediums, his focus remains the same: building coherent systems from simple components.

Between 2010–2020 he worked as lead origami educator at Óbudai Platán Library, teaching children, adults and educators through modular construction methods, visual thinking and three-dimensional structures.

Active member of the Hungarian Origami Circle (MOK).

Published in Ori-Magi magazine and represented in national exhibitions.
